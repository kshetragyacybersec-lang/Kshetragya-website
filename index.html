Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XIII · Verse 34

The Bhagavad Gita teaches that the Kshetragya possesses complete awareness: what is present, what is absent, what is hidden. Before we defend your network, we know it more completely than those who would attack it. Every vulnerability, every misconfiguration, every hidden passage. That is Kshetragya.

Tools surface the known. Our manual testing finds logic flaws and chained exploits that no automated scanner can enumerate — the ones that become the actual breach.

सत्Compliance-grade deliverables

धर्Domain-specialized ownership

Each partner owns one domain — network security, application security, or GRC. You engage a focused specialist, not a generalist reading about your problem for the first time.

Clear scope, clear deliverables, clear price before work begins. No retainer traps, no vague consulting hours, no surprises on your invoice.
