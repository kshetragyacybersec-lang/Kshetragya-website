Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XIII · Verse 34

The Bhagavad Gita teaches that the Kshetragya possesses complete awareness of the terrain: what is present, what is absent, and what is hidden. This is not a metaphor we borrowed. Before we defend your network, we know it more completely than those who would attack it — every vulnerability, every misconfiguration, every hidden passage. That is Kshetragya.

ज्ञाManual-first methodology

Automated scanners find the known. Our manual testing finds the logic flaws, chained misconfigurations, and attack paths no tool can enumerate — the ones that become the actual breach.

सत्Compliance-grade deliverables

Every report is built for its real audience — your auditor, your compliance officer, or the enterprise customer demanding proof of testing before signing your contract.

धर्Domain-specialized ownership

Each partner owns one domain — network security, application security, or GRC. You engage a focused specialist, not a generalist reading about your problem for the first time.

ऋतTransparent fixed engagements

Clear scope, clear deliverables, clear price before work begins. No retainer traps, no vague consulting hours, no surprises on your invoice.
